# Stakeholder discovery (pending real interview)

Record a genuine conversation before claiming that the model reflects Medisurge's operating priorities.

1. Which hospital types and locations can Medisurge currently serve?
2. Which of CSSD, NABH readiness, and modular OT is a current authorised offering?
3. What makes an account worth researching or approaching?
4. What data is currently hard to verify?
5. What is a realistic annual project capacity and sales cycle?
6. Which choices must remain with the owner or project lead?
7. Which part of an account brief would save preparation time?

Capture interview date, respondent role, permission to use answers, decision criteria, and what changed in the model. Keep identities and commercially sensitive answers private.
