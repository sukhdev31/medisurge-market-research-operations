# One claim, one source

The public source register starts empty because every included hospital account is fictional.

For a real account: resolve the exact legal or brand name and city; collect an official hospital or authority URL; write one atomic claim per register row; record the checked date; capture source ID on any expansion or quality rating; have a person check whether the page supports the exact claim. Use directories only to discover possible accounts. Treat a dated expansion article as a research signal, not proof of purchase intent. Mark conflicting claims explicitly and hold the account for review.

The app currently reads the repository's `public_data/source_register.csv`. Uploaded CSV account records are not automatically associated with an uploaded source register; keep unverified imported accounts in Research first until a private governed workflow exists. Do not upload patient, contact, proposal, price or other confidential data to the public demo.
